1 INTRODUCTION
1.1 This Civil Aviation Requirement lays down the minimum requirements for facilities to be provided by airlines to passengers who are denied boarding, whose flights are cancelled or delayed, or who are involuntarily downgraded.
1.2 This CAR is issued under the provisions of Rule 133A of the Aircraft Rules, 1937.

2 APPLICABILITY AND DEFINITIONS
2.1 Applicability. This CAR applies to all Indian scheduled operators in respect of domestic and international flights operated by them. It does not apply to flights operated by foreign carriers. It does not cover loss of, delay to or damage of baggage, which are governed by the Carriage by Air Act, 1972.
2.2 Definitions. For the purpose of this CAR, unless the context requires otherwise, the terms below have the meanings given.
2.2.1 Block time means the time from the moment the aircraft first moves for the purpose of taking off until the moment it comes to rest at the end of the flight, as published in the flight schedule.
2.2.2 Denied boarding means a refusal to carry a passenger on a flight although the passenger has presented himself for boarding under the conditions laid down in para 3.2.4, except where there are reasonable grounds to deny boarding such as reasons of health, safety or security, or inadequate travel documentation.
2.2.3 Cancellation means the non-operation of a flight which was previously planned and on which at least one seat was reserved.
2.2.4 Delay means the period by which the actual departure of a flight is later than its scheduled departure time as shown on the passenger's ticket.
2.2.5 Extraordinary circumstances means circumstances beyond the control of the airline which could not have been avoided even if all reasonable measures had been taken, including but not limited to political instability, natural disaster, civil war, insurrection or riot, flooding, explosion, government regulations, security risks, decisions of air traffic control, strikes affecting the operation of the airline, and meteorological conditions incompatible with the safe operation of the flight. Any other circumstance shall be treated as extraordinary only if it meets the general test in this definition.
2.2.6 Acceptable alternate flight means an alternate flight offered by the airline to the same destination which the passenger accepts, whether expressly or by travelling on it.
2.2.7 Downgrading means the carriage of a passenger in a class lower than the class for which the ticket was purchased, without the passenger's consent.
2.2.8 Contact details means the mobile number and e-mail address provided by the passenger at the time of booking.

3 REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Information to passengers
3.1.1 The airline shall collect contact details from every passenger at the time of booking and shall use them to inform the passenger of any cancellation or delay of the flight.
3.1.2 Where the passenger has not provided contact details at the time of booking, the airline shall not be liable for failure to inform the passenger of a cancellation or delay, and compensation that depends on the period of notice under para 3.3 shall not be payable.
3.2 Denied boarding
3.2.1 Where the airline expects to deny boarding, it shall first call for volunteers to surrender their seats in exchange for benefits agreed between the airline and the passenger.
3.2.2 Where boarding is denied involuntarily and the airline arranges an alternate flight scheduled to depart within one hour of the booked scheduled departure time, no compensation is payable.
3.2.3 Where boarding is denied involuntarily and the airline does not arrange an alternate flight within one hour, the airline shall pay compensation as follows: (a) 200 percent of the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, subject to a maximum of Rs 10,000, where the alternate flight departs within 24 hours of the booked departure; (b) 400 percent of the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, subject to a maximum of Rs 20,000, where the alternate flight departs more than 24 hours after the booked departure; (c) where no alternate flight is arranged or the passenger does not accept it, a full refund of the ticket in addition to compensation of 400 percent of the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, subject to a maximum of Rs 20,000.
3.2.4 The provisions of para 3.2.2 and 3.2.3 apply only where the passenger holds a confirmed reservation and has reported for check-in and boarding within the time stipulated by the airline. They do not apply where boarding is denied for reasons of health, safety or security, inadequate travel documentation, or where a change of aircraft of lesser capacity is made for operational or safety reasons.
3.3 Cancellation
3.3.1 The airline shall inform passengers of a cancellation at least two weeks before the scheduled time of departure and arrange an alternate flight or refund the ticket, as acceptable to the passenger; in that case no compensation is payable. Where the passenger is informed less than two weeks and more than 24 hours before the scheduled time of departure, the airline shall offer an alternate flight departing within two hours of the booked scheduled departure time or refund the ticket, as acceptable to the passenger; where it does neither, compensation as per para 3.3.2 is payable.
3.3.2 Where the passenger is informed of the cancellation less than 24 hours before the scheduled time of departure, including at the airport, the airline shall provide either an acceptable alternate flight, or compensation in addition to a full refund of the ticket, as follows: (a) Rs 5,000 or the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, whichever is less, for a flight of block time up to and including one hour; (b) Rs 7,500 or the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, whichever is less, for a flight of block time of more than one hour and up to and including two hours; (c) Rs 10,000 or the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, whichever is less, for a flight of block time of more than two hours. Passengers waiting for the alternate flight shall be provided facilities as per para 3.6.
3.3.3 The compensation under para 3.3.1 and 3.3.2 shall not be payable where the cancellation is caused by extraordinary circumstances as defined in para 2.2.5, subject to para 3.7.
3.3.4 Where the passenger has been informed of the cancellation through the contact details provided under para 3.1.1, the period of notice shall be counted from the time the information was sent.
3.4 Delays
3.4.1 Where the airline expects a delay beyond its scheduled departure time of two hours or more for flights of block time up to two and a half hours, three hours or more for flights of block time of more than two and a half hours and up to five hours, and four hours or more for all other flights, it shall offer meals and refreshments in relation to the waiting time, free of charge, as per para 3.6.
3.4.2 Where the delay is expected to be more than six hours and the passenger has been informed less than 24 hours before the scheduled departure, the airline shall offer an alternate flight or a full refund of the ticket, as acceptable to the passenger.
3.4.3 Where the delay is expected to be more than 24 hours, or more than six hours for a flight scheduled to depart between 2000 hours and 0300 hours, the airline shall provide hotel accommodation, including transfers, free of charge, as per para 3.6.
3.4.4 No monetary compensation is payable for a delay under this CAR. The passenger's entitlement in case of delay is limited to the facilities, alternate flight and refund set out in para 3.4.1 to 3.4.3.
3.5 Downgrading
3.5.1 Where a passenger holding a confirmed ticket on a domestic flight is involuntarily downgraded, the airline shall refund 75 percent of the ticket cost of that sector, including taxes, and shall carry the passenger in the next available class free of charge.
3.5.2 Where a passenger on an international flight is involuntarily downgraded, the airline shall refund, of the ticket cost of that sector including taxes: (a) 30 percent for a sector of 1500 km or less; (b) 50 percent for a sector of more than 1500 km and up to 3500 km; (c) 75 percent for a sector of more than 3500 km.
3.6 Facilities
3.6.1 Meals and refreshments shall be provided free of charge in relation to the waiting time to passengers who are waiting for a delayed flight or for an alternate flight following a cancellation or denied boarding.
3.6.2 Hotel accommodation, including transfers, shall be provided free of charge where the waiting time requires an overnight stay as per para 3.4.3.
3.7 Extraordinary circumstances
3.7.1 The airline shall not be obliged to pay compensation under para 3.2 and 3.3 where the denied boarding or cancellation is caused by extraordinary circumstances as defined in para 2.2.5.
3.7.2 The burden of proving extraordinary circumstances lies with the airline. Where the passenger disputes the airline's attribution, the claim shall be decided on the evidence and not on the airline's statement alone.
3.7.3 The facilities under para 3.6 shall be provided irrespective of whether the disruption is caused by extraordinary circumstances.
3.8 Refunds
3.8.1 Refunds shall be processed within seven days for credit card payments and immediately for cash payments; for payments through travel agents the refund shall be the responsibility of the airline.
3.9 Grievance redressal
3.9.1 Every airline shall appoint a nodal officer to receive passenger grievances. Every decision declining a claim shall state the provision of this CAR under which it is declined.
3.9.2 A passenger not satisfied with the airline's decision may escalate the grievance through the AirSewa portal of the Ministry of Civil Aviation.
